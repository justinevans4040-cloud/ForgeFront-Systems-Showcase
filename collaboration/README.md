# Work With ForgeFront

**ForgeFront builds agentic systems, vertical software, and automation designed to survive contact with real work.**

We are interested in collaboration when combining strengths creates a better product, a faster path to market, or an opportunity neither side should attack alone.

## Where ForgeFront fits

### You have the market. We bring capability.
If you have customers, distribution, a strong product surface, or deep industry access, ForgeFront can explore how specialized agentic or workflow capability could strengthen the offering.

### You have the product. We make the hard parts smarter.
ForgeFront can explore integrations where structured intelligence, automation, and purpose-built operator workflows create meaningful leverage.

### We build something together.
For the right opportunity, ForgeFront is open to joint product development that combines product design, engineering, domain knowledge, distribution, and agentic systems.

## The boundary is deliberate

This repository shows enough to evaluate ForgeFront's capability and whether there is real overlap.

Proprietary source, prompts, protected architecture, internal agent topology, workflow contracts, credentials, private data, and implementation details stay private unless there is a legitimate business reason and appropriate terms for deeper technical disclosure.
