# Respawn Academy — Master Thread Record

**Purpose:** Complete project record assembled from the Respawn planning thread and the preserved legacy-site audit.

**Domain:** https://respawnacademy.com/

**Status:** Legacy site preserved. Product direction expanded from a school discipline tool into a privacy-first, accessible, lifelong learning and growth system.

## Core idea

Respawn is a game-based learning, recovery, communication, and personal-growth system that can begin in early childhood and grow with a person through school, graduation, and adulthood.

It is not designed to punish children, diagnose children, exploit families, or separate children into lesser experiences. It gives children a structured way to learn, rebuild, communicate, practice, and progress—with a trusted Guardian that adapts to how they learn.

## Product shape: the Respawn Quad

1. **Respawn Academy** — the child-facing game and learning world.
2. **Respawn Guardian** — the parent/family companion for support, configuration, and visibility boundaries.
3. **Respawn AI Mentor** — an adaptive tutor, coach, explainer, and summer learning companion.
4. **Respawn Arena** — safe cooperative, competitive, and seasonal game experiences.

The Quad supports school-year use, summer learning, family participation, and long-term progression without pay-to-win mechanics.

## No pay-to-win

There are no paid capability tiers. Money cannot purchase XP, rank, victories, stronger tutoring, better AI, accessibility, or educational advantage.

There may be lifecycle stages and progression systems, but they represent growth and continuity—not payment levels.

The family supports the journey. The graduate owns what they built.

## Respawn Covenant

A child who maintains Respawn from early school years through adulthood receives the complete personal system free for life at 18.

Starting in kindergarten does not mean the child finishes sooner. It means the Guardian has longer continuity: it can learn how the person learns, what motivates them, where they struggle, and what helps them recover.

At the Sovereign Handoff:

- The family subscription ends.
- Parent access ends.
- The graduate receives the Sovereign Key.
- Their data and Guardian become exclusively theirs.
- The complete personal system remains free for life.

This should be a contractual product promise supported by the architecture and business model—not merely marketing language.

## Privacy and encryption: non-negotiable

Privacy and encryption are part of the Respawn Constitution.

- Encrypt data on devices, in transit, in storage, and in backups.
- Use a local-first design wherever practical.
- Never sell child data or advertise against it.
- Never use a child’s history to train outside AI models without explicit, meaningful authorization.
- Collect only what the system needs.
- Separate child, parent, school, educator, and administrator identities.
- Use role-based access and visible permission boundaries.
- Do not provide secret surveillance.
- Do not include hidden microphones, manipulative engagement, or dark patterns.
- Provide export and permanent deletion.
- Provide safe key recovery without creating a universal company backdoor.
- Log exceptional access in an immutable audit trail.
- At adulthood, terminate parental access and transfer control to the graduate.

Governing rule:

> Respawn may learn for the child, but it may never exploit what it learns about the child.

## Inclusive design and communication access

Do not create a separate “special-ed Respawn.” Build one Respawn with an **Adaptive Access Layer** available to every child at no additional cost.

The respectful communication category is **AAC — Augmentative and Alternative Communication**. It can include typed text, symbols, images, selectable responses, generated speech, switches, eye-gaze systems, and compatibility with external AAC devices.

Use the product language **Personalized Access Support** and **Personalized Access Profile**, not “special cases.”

Access settings belong directly beside every other onboarding and personalization setting:

- Communication method
- Typed, spoken, symbol, or selectable responses
- Text-to-speech and speech-to-text
- AAC compatibility
- Reading level and instruction length
- Visual, audio, and haptic guidance
- Sensory intensity and reduced-stimulation mode
- Interaction method and motor-access controls
- Lesson pacing, repetition, and break frequency
- Routine structure and transition warnings
- Language and translation preferences
- Parent-assisted configuration
- Personalized Access Support requests

No diagnosis, documentation, or extra payment should be required for ordinary access configuration. Respawn may adapt to what helps without diagnosing or labeling the child.

> Every child deserves a voice—even when that voice is not spoken.

Parents are partners, not applicants. Children are participants, not diagnoses.

## Parent and child dignity

Parents often know what helps their child better than an automated profile. Respawn can recommend settings, but it must not dismiss, overrule, or patronize families.

The child’s growing independence must also be respected. Parent visibility is not unlimited surveillance. The system must clearly show what is shared, with whom, and why.

## Continuum and ownership

The Guardian can evolve through a continuity model:

- **Origin Core** — early childhood foundation
- **Forge Core** — early teen development
- **Vanguard Core** — late teen independence
- **Sovereign Core** — adult-owned system

The future possibility is a user-owned Guardian Identity Package that can move between approved devices or future robotics platforms. This must never be marketed as consciousness or a transfer of a human mind.

## Antigravity “What & Why” dropdown

Create an accordion/dropdown titled **What is Respawn—and why does it exist?** Make it a primary, readable explanation—not a hidden disclaimer.

Suggested panels:

### What is Respawn?

Respawn is a game-based learning and growth system that helps children practice skills, recover from setbacks, communicate in ways that work for them, and build confidence over time. It connects the child experience, family support, adaptive mentoring, and safe game-world progression.

### Why was it created?

Children are often removed, labeled, or reset without being given the tools to understand what happened or try again. Respawn replaces shame and disconnection with structure, practice, accountability, support, and a genuine path forward.

### Why does continuity matter?

A Guardian that grows with a child can become more helpful without owning the child. It can remember what explanations work, what pacing helps, and how the child prefers to communicate—while keeping the child and family in control of that history.

### What about children who communicate or learn differently?

Respawn includes an Adaptive Access Layer for every child. Families can configure AAC, generated speech, typed or symbol-based responses, sensory controls, pacing, repetition, motor access, and Personalized Access Support without diagnosis, stigma, or additional cost.

### Who owns the data?

The child and family—not advertisers, not data brokers, and not an invisible platform. Data is encrypted, access is limited and visible, and the child receives ownership of their Guardian and history at adulthood.

### What happens at 18?

The Sovereign Handoff ends parent access and gives the adult the Sovereign Key. The complete personal system remains free for life for participants who maintained the Respawn journey.

### Is it pay-to-win?

Never. Payment cannot buy rank, XP, stronger AI, better learning, or accessibility. Progress comes from participation, learning, effort, recovery, and teamwork.

## Legacy-site archive findings

The current live site is a compact static Netlify deployment with one HTML page, one CSS file, one JavaScript file, and external Google Fonts. It contains no image assets.

The old site presents Respawn primarily as a K–12 restorative-discipline platform with three intervention tiers. That remains useful as a school-facing **Respawn Recovery** component, but it should no longer define the entire product.

Current live claims and wiring that require revision:

- “100% Admin Visibility” conflicts with the Privacy Covenant.
- The homepage leads with discipline rather than the child’s lifelong journey.
- The three tiers appear to define the whole product.
- Accessibility and Personalized Access Support are absent.
- Parent partnership is underrepresented.
- The Guardian, game world, summer tutor, Sovereign Handoff, and lifetime ownership promise are absent.
- The Student App and Admin Dashboard links point to public Perplexity Computer projects.
- The demo form is simulated only: it validates in the browser, waits 1.2 seconds, clears the fields, and shows success. It sends no email, API request, or database record.
